Formatting

Select text or position the cursor, then click a color or formatting button to insert its code.

Use § codes (for minecraft formatting). Converted into RichText in the output at bottom of page.

The selected font is added to the generated RichText and applied to the whole preview.

BuilderIcons

Choose an icon name, then insert it at the text cursor. Enable Filled to use the bold BuilderIcons variant. The icon names here are examples, not a complete icon catalogue.

BuilderIcons are Roblox-specific fonts and may appear as ordinary text in your browser preview.
